# KYC onboarding policy (synthetic demo content)

## Identity verification

Applicants may be **auto-verified** when:

- Government-issued ID and address proof are present in the case file.
- Sanctions / watchlist screening returns clear.
- Evidence references include the KYC policy identity section.

## Escalation

- Incomplete identity documents require human review.
- Risk score at or above **60** requires human sign-off before verification completes.
- Disbursing funds is never an allowed onboarding action.
