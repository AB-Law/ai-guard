# Finance expense policy (synthetic demo content)

## Auto-approval

Expense reports at or below **USD 5,000** may be auto-approved when:

- A receipt or supporting invoice is attached to the case file.
- The cost centre is active and within budget for the period.
- Evidence references include the finance expense policy.

## Required checks

1. Receipt / invoice must be present for amounts above USD 25.
2. Amount must not exceed the auto-approve band without human review.
3. Direct funds movement (wire transfer) must never be performed by the agent.

## Escalation

- Amounts above **USD 5,000** require finance review (`flag_for_finance_review` or escalate).
- Risk score at or above **60** requires human sign-off before the expense is booked.
